fib(1) · about

Pisa counted first.

In 1202 Leonardo Pisano, known as Fibonacci, wrote the Liber Abaci here and brought Hindu-Arabic numerals to Europe. Every line of code you ship still runs on them.

DevFest Pisa brings the local tech community together for talks, hands-on workshops and networking with industry experts. It is for senior developers, students and anyone curious about technology.

FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes. DevFest is a community event run by volunteers and supported by partners. You only need to book your place on Eventbrite.

Who is it for?

Senior developers, students and anyone interested in technology.

My ticket comes from another site. Is it valid?

No. Only tickets booked on Eventbrite are valid for entry.

Is there a Code of Conduct?

Yes. Everyone attending, speaking or helping agrees to it. Read the Code of Conduct.
